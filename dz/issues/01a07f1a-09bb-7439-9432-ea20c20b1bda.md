A conditional branch immediately followed by its complement is an
unconditional transfer: one of the two is always taken, and nothing between
them can change the flags because there is nothing between them. Its
fall-through is therefore unreachable.

The jump-tracing disassembler does not model this, so it decodes whatever
follows the pair. In `decoded/neptune/testdata/neptune.b33` that is padding,
and the cost is out of proportion to the idiom:

    0E71: INX
    0E72: BNE $0E12
    0E74: BEQ $0E12      <- together, unconditional
    0E76: DEX
    0E77: BNE $0E12
    0E79: BEQ $0E12      <- together, unconditional
    0E7B: 00 00 ...      <- padding, decoded as BRK

Neptune has 8 such pairs: $0E72, $0E77, $0E8E, $0F72, $0F77, $0F8E, $1B5C,
$22BF.

## What it costs

Decoding the padding as `BRK` pulls in the whole interrupt path, because
apple2tc models `BRK` as a call to a synthesised interrupt-entry block:

- **11 bogus `BRK` instructions** at $0E7B-$0E81, $0F7B-$0F81, $117F,
  $338C-$338E.
- **40 of the 48 ROM instructions** in the listing -- $DB5C, $FA40-$FA56,
  $FDED, $FF4C-$FF58 -- none of which the recording ever executed. The only
  ROM the game really calls is $FCA8 (MON_WAIT).
- **2 of the 13 routine-candidate rejections**: the synthesised interrupt
  block itself (reported as `REJECT $10100 terminator JmpInd`) and the
  cascade `REJECT $3340 invalid JSR to $10100`.
- Probably some of the 6 `invalid instruction` warnings; $22BF's dead
  fall-through is at $22C3 and one warning is at $22CD.

## Suggested fix

In the tracer, when a conditional branch's immediately preceding instruction
is its complement (`BNE`/`BEQ`, `BCC`/`BCS`, `BMI`/`BPL`, `BVC`/`BVS`) and the
two are adjacent, do not queue the fall-through.

The targets do not have to match for the reasoning to hold -- `BNE X` followed
by `BEQ Y` is still exhaustive -- though in Neptune all 8 pairs share a
target.

## Blast radius

Small. `decoded/snake-byte/as-generated/snake-byte.lst` contains **zero**
complementary pairs, so nothing there changes.
